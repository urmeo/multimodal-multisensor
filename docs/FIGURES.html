Figure provenance

The saved figures and TOML provenance files are retained. Their analysis code is preserved in snapshot 0f2b1cc. The analysis scripts are no longer included in this checkout.

Recording figures

The heart-rate, interval and pupil plots use data/case-study/raw/hr_01.txt, ibi_01.txt and sed_01.txt directly. They describe the available samples from session 1.

Heart rate retains finite positive readings with confidence 1. Intervals retain finite positive readings, including repeated samples. Pupils retain finite positive readings with 0.5 < pupilQ <= 1. Channels stay separate. Each stream uses its own recording-relative time in seconds; clocks are not aligned across streams. Reported units are bpm, ms and mm. Calibration and unique-beat provenance are unverified.

outputs/recording-figures.toml records source hashes, a hash of the exact selected values, filters, units, counts and image hashes. The archived recording-figure script records the calculation. These plots do not establish anxiety categories or validate the ten-person group tables.

The session photograph appears in thesis Annex E, PDF page 46, printed page 49. Its caption explicitly records photo consent. The experimental setup photograph is also preserved. Source image bytes are unchanged.

Historical group-summary calculations

The README's reliability plot and the correlation plot use the three unchanged group tables. ICC(1,1) estimates have 95% confidence intervals calculated from the F distribution. Spearman correlations use ten participant rows and show no significance stars.

Most group rows cannot be traced to the available raw recordings. The calculations reproduce the released tables; they do not independently verify the cohort measurements.

outputs/figures.csv records calculated values. outputs/figures.toml records source hashes, methods and figure hashes. The archived group-figure script records the calculation.

Historical snapshots

Historical images retain their bytes from release baseline a369ee0dc2dcac24feaa9e2e1faf160c39d5a3de. Plots are in outputs/; photographs and screenshots are in images/. The baseline revised the recruitment image; its hash below identifies that file. Acquisition and settings hashes are unavailable for these images.

  • The former README heatmap shows interval S1/S2 = 0.03 and interval S1/pupil S3 = 0.85. The separately released Spearman image shows 0.55 and 0.48. Differences in methods or source snapshots prevent treating the images as interchangeable.
  • The silhouette figure peaks at k = 2, while the cluster display uses k = 3. These historical images do not establish anxiety categories or validated participant clusters. They also do not establish that PCA preceded clustering.
  • Timing, pupil, fixation and scoring screenshots retain their historical labels and source values. The saved descriptive results exclude unsupported clinical cutoffs and mixed units.
